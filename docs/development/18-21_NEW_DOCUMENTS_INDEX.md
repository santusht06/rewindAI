## Mandatory diagram study
Open and study the four `.mmd` files in `../system_design_diagrams/` with prefixes 18, 19, 20, and 21 before implementation. Also read `17_REWIND_CROSS_APP_ASSISTANT_PRODUCT_REQUIREMENT.md`.
